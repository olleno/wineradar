    Wineries in Göttlesbrunn

    Göttlesbrunn has 22 wine producers in our guide in Carnuntum. 20 of them welcome visitors, and 8 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Zweigelt, Merlot, Blaufränkisch and Chardonnay. Within walking distance there are 8 places to eat and drink and 2 places to stay.
