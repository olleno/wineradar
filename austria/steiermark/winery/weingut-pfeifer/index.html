    Weingut Pfeifer

    Weingut Pfeifer is a wine producer in Sankt Anna am Aigen, in the Vulkanland Steiermark wine area of Styria (Steiermark). The estate farms about 10 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are white, led by Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc and Weissburgunder. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
