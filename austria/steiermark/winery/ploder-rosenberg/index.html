    Ploder-Rosenberg

    Ploder-Rosenberg is a wine producer in Sankt Peter am Ottersbach, in the Vulkanland Steiermark wine area of Styria (Steiermark). The estate farms about 11 hectares of vineyard, organically. Its rated wines are white, led by Souvignier Gris, Sauvignon Blanc and Grauburgunder. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
