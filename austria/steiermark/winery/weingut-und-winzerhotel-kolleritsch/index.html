    Weingut und Winzerhotel Kolleritsch

    Weingut und Winzerhotel Kolleritsch is a wine producer in Tieschen, in the Vulkanland Steiermark wine area of Styria (Steiermark). Its rated wines are white, led by Chardonnay, Grauburgunder and Weissburgunder.
