    Weingut Leitgeb

    Weingut Leitgeb is a wine producer in Bad Gleichenberg, in the Vulkanland Steiermark wine area of Styria (Steiermark). Its rated wines are mostly white, led by Sauvignon Blanc, Weissburgunder and Chardonnay.
