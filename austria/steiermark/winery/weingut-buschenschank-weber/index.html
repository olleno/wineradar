    Weingut & Buschenschank Weber

    Weingut & Buschenschank Weber is a wine producer in Sankt Stefan ob Stainz, in the Weststeiermark wine area of Styria (Steiermark). Its rated wines are mostly rosé, led by Blauer Wildbacher and Gelber Muskateller. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
