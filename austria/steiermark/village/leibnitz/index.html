    Wineries in Leibnitz

    Leibnitz has 8 wine producers in our guide, in the Südsteiermark area. 7 of them welcome visitors, and 2 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay, Weissburgunder and Grauburgunder. Within walking distance there are 36 places to eat and drink and 12 places to stay.
