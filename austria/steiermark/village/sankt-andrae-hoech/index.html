    Wineries in Sankt Andrä-Höch

    Sankt Andrä-Höch has 3 wine producers in our guide, in the Südsteiermark area. 2 of them welcome visitors, and 2 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Sauvignon Blanc, Grauburgunder, Chardonnay and Welschriesling. Within walking distance there are 6 places to eat and drink and 2 places to stay.
