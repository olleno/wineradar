    Wineries in Fehring

    Fehring has 4 wine producers in our guide, in the Vulkanland Steiermark area. 2 of them welcome visitors. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Sauvignon Blanc, Weissburgunder, Chardonnay and Riesling. Within walking distance there are 15 places to eat and drink and 1 places to stay.
