    Südsteiermark wineries

    The steep hills along the Slovenian border around Gamlitz, Ehrenhausen, Leutschach and the Sausal. With 2,798 hectares it is Styria’s largest area; Sauvignon Blanc covers about 26%, followed by Welschriesling, Muskateller and Weißburgunder. The Sausal around Kitzeck has some of Austria’s steepest vineyards and is known for Riesling. Südsteiermark DAC since 2018.
