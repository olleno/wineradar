    Wine area · Vulkanland Steiermark DAC

    Vulkanland Steiermark wineries

    Eastern Styria between old volcanic cones such as Riegersburg, Kapfenstein, Straden and Klöch. 1,657 hectares, mostly white: Welschriesling and Weißburgunder lead, then Sauvignon Blanc and Muskateller, and Klöch is known for its Traminer. Vulkanland Steiermark DAC since 2018.
