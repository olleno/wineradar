    Baumhausweine

    Baumhausweine is a wine producer in Gobelsburg, Kamptal, Lower Austria. The estate farms about 20 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are white, led by Grüner Veltliner and Riesling. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
