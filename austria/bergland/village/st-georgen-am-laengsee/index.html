    Wineries in St. Georgen am Längsee

    St. Georgen am Längsee has 3 wine producers in our guide, in the Kärnten area. 2 of them welcome visitors, and 1 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Pinot Noir, Sauvignon Blanc, Grauburgunder and Grüner Veltliner. Within walking distance there are 7 places to eat and drink and 4 places to stay.
