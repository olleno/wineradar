    Weinbau Gmeiner

    Weinbau Gmeiner is a wine producer in Perg, in the Oberösterreich wine area of Bergland (Carinthia, Upper Austria, Tyrol, Vorarlberg, Salzburg). The estate farms about 1 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Zweigelt and Rösler. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
