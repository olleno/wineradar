    Wineries in Feuersbrunn

    Feuersbrunn has 14 wine producers in our guide in the Wagram. 12 of them welcome visitors, and 2 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Grüner Veltliner, Roter Veltliner, Riesling and Chardonnay. Within walking distance there are 8 places to eat and drink and 2 places to stay.
