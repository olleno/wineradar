    Weinbau Familie Meißner

    Weinbau Familie Meißner is a wine producer in Weidling, Wagram, Lower Austria. The estate farms about 2.4 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Zweigelt. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
