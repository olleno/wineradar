    Großwiesendorf · Wagram

    Weinbau Familie Stadler

    Weinbau Familie Stadler is a wine producer in Großwiesendorf, Wagram, Lower Austria. The estate farms about 7 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are mostly white, led by Roter Veltliner, Grüner Veltliner and Zweigelt. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
