    Pinot Noir in the Weinviertel

    25 producers in the Weinviertel make Pinot Noir that has been rated, and for 1 of them it is the leading grape. The villages with the most producers are Poysdorf (2), Kleinhöflein (2), Unterretzbach (2), Röschitz (1), Seefeld-Kadolz (1).
