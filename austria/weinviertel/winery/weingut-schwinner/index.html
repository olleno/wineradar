    Weingut Schwinner

    Weingut Schwinner is a wine producer in Hohenwarth, Weinviertel, Lower Austria. The estate farms about 10 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are white, led by Grüner Veltliner and Weissburgunder. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
