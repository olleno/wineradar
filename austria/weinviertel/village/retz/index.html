    Wineries in Retz

    Retz has 12 wine producers in our guide in the Weinviertel. 3 of them welcome visitors, and 1 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Grüner Veltliner, Merlot, Zweigelt and Gelber Muskateller. Within walking distance there are 17 places to eat and drink and 7 places to stay.
