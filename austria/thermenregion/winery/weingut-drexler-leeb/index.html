    Weingut Drexler-Leeb

    Weingut Drexler-Leeb is a wine producer in Perchtoldsdorf, Thermenregion, Lower Austria. The estate farms about 10 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red and white, led by Zweigelt, St. Laurent and Rotgipfler. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
