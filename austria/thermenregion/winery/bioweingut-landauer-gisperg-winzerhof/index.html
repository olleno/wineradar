    Tattendorf · Thermenregion

    Bioweingut Landauer-Gisperg Winzerhof

    Bioweingut Landauer-Gisperg Winzerhof is a wine producer in Tattendorf, Thermenregion, Lower Austria. The estate farms about 30 hectares of vineyard, organically. Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Pinot Noir, St. Laurent and Rotgipfler. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
