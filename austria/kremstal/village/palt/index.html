    Wineries in Palt

    Palt has 10 wine producers in our guide in the Kremstal. 7 of them welcome visitors, and 3 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Grüner Veltliner, Riesling, Gelber Muskateller and Weissburgunder. Within walking distance there are 8 places to eat and drink and 3 places to stay.
