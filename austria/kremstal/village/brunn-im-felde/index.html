    Wineries in Brunn im Felde

    Brunn im Felde has 4 wine producers in our guide in the Kremstal. 4 of them welcome visitors, and 1 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Riesling, Grüner Veltliner, Roter Veltliner and Gelber Muskateller.
