    Cabernet Sauvignon in Burgenland

    128 producers in Burgenland make Cabernet Sauvignon that has been rated, and for 9 of them it is the leading grape. The villages with the most producers are Gols (17), Deutschkreutz (14), Jois (7), Eisenstadt (6), Neckenmarkt (5).
