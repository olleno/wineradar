    Chardonnay in Burgenland

    217 producers in Burgenland make Chardonnay that has been rated, and for 65 of them it is the leading grape. The villages with the most producers are Gols (33), Rust (20), Illmitz (16), Apetlon (13), Purbach am Neusiedler See (13).
