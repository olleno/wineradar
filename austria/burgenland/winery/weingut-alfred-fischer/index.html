    Weingut Alfred Fischer

    Weingut Alfred Fischer is a wine producer in Stöttera, in the Rosalia wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 12 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Blaufränkisch, Zweigelt and Sauvignon Blanc. Wine can be bought directly at the estate.
