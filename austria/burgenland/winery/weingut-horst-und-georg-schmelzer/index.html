Weingut Horst und Georg Schmelzer

Weingut Horst und Georg Schmelzer is a wine producer in Gols, in the Neusiedlersee wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 15 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Zweigelt, Weissburgunder and Grüner Veltliner. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
