    Weingut Toni Hartl

    Weingut Toni Hartl is a wine producer in Reisenberg, in the Leithaberg wine area of Lower Austria, just across the border from Burgenland. The estate farms about 25 hectares of vineyard, biodynamically. Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Blaufränkisch, Chardonnay and Syrah. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
