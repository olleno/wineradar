    Zantho

    Zantho is a wine producer in Andau, in the Neusiedlersee wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 80 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red and white, led by Grüner Veltliner, Scheurebe and Merlot. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
