    Weinbau Schweifer

    Weinbau Schweifer is a wine producer in Eisenstadt, in the Leithaberg wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 8.5 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Zweigelt, Pinot Noir and Blaufränkisch. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
