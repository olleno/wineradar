Leithaprodersdorf · Leithaberg DAC

Weingut Menitz

Weingut Menitz is a wine producer in Leithaprodersdorf, in the Leithaberg wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 2.7 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Blaufränkisch, Chardonnay and Grüner Veltliner. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
