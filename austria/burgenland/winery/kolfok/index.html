    Neckenmarkt · Mittelburgenland DAC

    Kolfok

    Kolfok is a wine producer in Neckenmarkt, in the Mittelburgenland wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The vineyards are farmed organically. Its rated wines are mostly white, led by Blaufränkisch, Furmint and Grüner Veltliner. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
