    WEIN-Träumerei IBY-LEHRNER

    WEIN-Träumerei IBY-LEHRNER is a wine producer in Horitschon, in the Mittelburgenland wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 18 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Blaufränkisch, Cabernet Sauvignon and Zweigelt. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
