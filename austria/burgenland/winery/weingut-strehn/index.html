    Weingut Strehn

    Weingut Strehn is a wine producer in Deutschkreutz, in the Mittelburgenland wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The vineyards are converting to organic farming. Its rated wines are mostly rosé, led by Blaufränkisch, Chardonnay and Cabernet Franc. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
