Weingut Familie Weber

Weingut Familie Weber is a wine producer in Lutzmannsburg, in the Mittelburgenland wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 12 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Blaufränkisch and Merlot. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
