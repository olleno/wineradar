    Weingut Polczer

    Weingut Polczer is a wine producer in Eisenberg an der Pinka, in the Eisenberg wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 20 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Blaufränkisch and Zweigelt. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
