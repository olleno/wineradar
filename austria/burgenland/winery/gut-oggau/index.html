Gut Oggau

Gut Oggau is a wine producer in Oggau am Neusiedler See, in the Leithaberg wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The vineyards are farmed organically. Its rated wines are red and white, led by Blaufränkisch, Gewürztraminer and Grüner Veltliner. The producer welcomes visitors.
