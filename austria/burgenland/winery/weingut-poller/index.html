    Weingut Poller

    Weingut Poller is a wine producer in Deutsch Schützen-Eisenberg, in the Eisenberg wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 11.3 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Blaufränkisch, Merlot and Cabernet Sauvignon. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
