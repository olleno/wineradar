Weingut Christian Tschida

Weingut Christian Tschida is a wine producer in Illmitz, in the Neusiedlersee wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The vineyards are farmed organically. Its rated wines are mostly white, led by Chardonnay, Weissburgunder and Syrah.
