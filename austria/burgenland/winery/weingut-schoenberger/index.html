Weingut Schönberger

Weingut Schönberger is a wine producer in Mörbisch am See, in the Leithaberg wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 22 hectares of vineyard, biodynamically. Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Blaufränkisch, Neuburger and Weissburgunder. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
