    Rechnitz · Eisenberg DAC

    Weingut Batthyány

    Weingut Batthyány is a wine producer in Rechnitz, in the Eisenberg wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 3.5 hectares of vineyard. The vineyards are converting to organic farming. Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Blaufränkisch, Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
