    Weingut Anton und Gabriele Gober

    Weingut Anton und Gabriele Gober is a wine producer in Horitschon, in the Mittelburgenland wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 5.5 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Blaufränkisch, Zweigelt and Pinot Noir. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
