    Schmelzer's

    Schmelzer's is a wine producer in Gols, in the Neusiedlersee wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 15 hectares of vineyard, biodynamically. Its rated wines are red, led by Zweigelt, Cabernet Sauvignon and Weissburgunder. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
