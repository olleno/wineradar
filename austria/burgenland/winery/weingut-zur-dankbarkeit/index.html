Weingut Zur Dankbarkeit

Weingut Zur Dankbarkeit is a wine producer in Podersdorf am See, in the Neusiedlersee wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 10 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red and white, led by Welschriesling, Grauburgunder and Chardonnay. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
