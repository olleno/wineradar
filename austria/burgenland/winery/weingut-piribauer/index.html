    Weingut Piribauer

    Weingut Piribauer is a wine producer in Neudörfl, in the Rosalia wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 18 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are mostly rosé, led by Blaufränkisch, Merlot and Chardonnay. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
