Weingut Juris

Weingut Juris is a wine producer in Gols, in the Neusiedlersee wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 18 hectares of vineyard. The vineyards are converting to organic farming. Its rated wines are red, led by Blaufränkisch, St. Laurent and Pinot Noir. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
