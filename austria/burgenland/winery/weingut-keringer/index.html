Weingut Keringer

Weingut Keringer is a wine producer in Mönchhof, in the Neusiedlersee wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 40 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Chardonnay, Merlot and Cabernet Sauvignon. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
