Eisenstadt · Leithaberg DAC

Weingut Hans Moser

Weingut Hans Moser is a wine producer in Eisenstadt, in the Leithaberg wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 16 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Blaufränkisch, Chardonnay and Merlot. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
