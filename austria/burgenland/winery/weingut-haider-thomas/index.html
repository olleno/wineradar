Neusiedl am See · Neusiedlersee DAC

Weingut Haider Thomas

Weingut Haider Thomas is a wine producer in Neusiedl am See, in the Neusiedlersee wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 12 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Malbec, Blaufränkisch and Tannat. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
