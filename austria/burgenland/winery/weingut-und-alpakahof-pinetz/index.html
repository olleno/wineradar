    Weingut und Alpakahof Pinetz

    Weingut und Alpakahof Pinetz is a wine producer in Frauenkirchen, in the Neusiedlersee wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 7.42 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Zweigelt, Syrah and Grauburgunder. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
