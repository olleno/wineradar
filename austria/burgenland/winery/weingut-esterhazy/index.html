    Weingut Esterházy

    Weingut Esterházy is a wine producer in Trausdorf an der Wulka, in the Leithaberg wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 65 hectares of vineyard, organically. Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Blaufränkisch, Chardonnay and Weissburgunder. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
