    Gols · Neusiedlersee DAC

    Weingut Andreas Gsellmann

    Weingut Andreas Gsellmann is a wine producer in Gols, in the Neusiedlersee wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 18.5 hectares of vineyard, biodynamically. Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Blaufränkisch, Weissburgunder and Scheurebe. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
