    Apetlon · Neusiedlersee DAC

    Weingut Velich

    Weingut Velich is a wine producer in Apetlon, in the Neusiedlersee wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 12 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are mostly white, led by Chardonnay, Scheurebe and Welschriesling. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
