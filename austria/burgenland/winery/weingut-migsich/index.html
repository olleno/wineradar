Weingut Migsich

Weingut Migsich is a wine producer in Antau, in the Rosalia wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 20 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Blaufränkisch, Merlot and Cabernet Sauvignon. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
