    Arachon T.FX.T

    Arachon T.FX.T is a wine producer in Pinkafeld, in the Mittelburgenland wine area of Burgenland, Austria. Its rated wines are red, led by Blaufränkisch. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
