    Weingut Eberherr

    Weingut Eberherr is a wine producer in St. Margarethen im Burgenland, in the Leithaberg wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 12 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Blaufränkisch, Cabernet Sauvignon and Chardonnay. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
