    Weingut Wurzinger Pia

    Weingut Wurzinger Pia is a wine producer in Tadten, in the Neusiedlersee wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 25 hectares of vineyard, organically. Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Merlot, Chardonnay and Blaufränkisch. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
