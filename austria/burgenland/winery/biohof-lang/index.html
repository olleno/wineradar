    Biohof Lang

    Biohof Lang is a wine producer in Halbturn, in the Neusiedlersee wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The vineyards are farmed organically. Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Cabernet Sauvignon, Chardonnay and Zweigelt. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
