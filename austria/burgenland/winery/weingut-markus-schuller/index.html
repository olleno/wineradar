    Weingut Markus Schuller

    Weingut Markus Schuller is a wine producer in Oggau am Neusiedler See, in the Leithaberg wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 12 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Blaufränkisch, Zweigelt and Chardonnay. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
