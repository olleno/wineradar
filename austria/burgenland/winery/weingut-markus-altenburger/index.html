    Weingut Markus Altenburger

    Weingut Markus Altenburger is a wine producer in Jois, in the Leithaberg wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 17 hectares of vineyard, organically. Its rated wines are red, led by Blaufränkisch and Zweigelt. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
