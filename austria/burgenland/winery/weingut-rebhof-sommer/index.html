    Weingut Rebhof Sommer

    Weingut Rebhof Sommer is a wine producer in Mörbisch am See, in the Leithaberg wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 8.5 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Syrah, Merlot and Blaufränkisch. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
