Weinhof Gassler

Weinhof Gassler is a wine producer in Moschendorf, in the Eisenberg wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 4.3 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Blaufränkisch and Zweigelt. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
