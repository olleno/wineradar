Weingut Haider

Weingut Haider is a wine producer in Illmitz, in the Neusiedlersee wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The vineyards are farmed organically. Its rated wines are sweet, led by Scheurebe, Welschriesling and Chardonnay. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
