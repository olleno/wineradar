    Weingut Liszt

    Weingut Liszt is a wine producer in Leithaprodersdorf, in the Leithaberg wine area of Burgenland, Austria. The estate farms about 7 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Blaufränkisch, Cabernet Sauvignon and Zweigelt. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
