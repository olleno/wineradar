    Wineries in Pamhagen

    Pamhagen has 3 wine producers in our guide, in the Neusiedlersee area. 1 of them welcome visitors, and 1 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Zweigelt, Grauburgunder, Cabernet Sauvignon and St. Laurent. Within walking distance there are 4 places to eat and drink and 2 places to stay.
