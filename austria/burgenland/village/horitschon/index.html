    Wineries in Horitschon

    Horitschon has 20 wine producers in our guide, in the Mittelburgenland area. 10 of them welcome visitors, and 3 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Blaufränkisch, Zweigelt, Merlot and Cabernet Sauvignon. Within walking distance there are 4 places to eat and drink and 1 places to stay.
