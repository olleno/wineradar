    Wineries in Jois

    Jois has 28 wine producers in our guide, in the Neusiedlersee area. 13 of them welcome visitors, and 5 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Blaufränkisch, Zweigelt, Cabernet Sauvignon and Chardonnay. Within walking distance there are 7 places to eat and drink and 6 places to stay.
