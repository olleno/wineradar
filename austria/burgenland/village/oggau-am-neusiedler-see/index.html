    Wineries in Oggau am Neusiedler See

    Oggau am Neusiedler See has 10 wine producers in our guide, in the Leithaberg area. 4 of them welcome visitors, and 1 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Blaufränkisch, Zweigelt, Merlot and Chardonnay. Within walking distance there are 7 places to eat and drink and 2 places to stay.
