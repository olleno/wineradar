    Wineries in Mönchhof

    Mönchhof has 20 wine producers in our guide, in the Neusiedlersee area. 10 of them welcome visitors, and 1 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Zweigelt, Chardonnay, Merlot and St. Laurent. Within walking distance there are 7 places to eat and drink and 4 places to stay.
