Wineries in Donnerskirchen

Donnerskirchen has 11 wine producers in our guide, in the Leithaberg area. 5 of them welcome visitors, and 1 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Blaufränkisch, Chardonnay, Grüner Veltliner and Weissburgunder. Within walking distance there are 5 places to eat and drink and 4 places to stay.
