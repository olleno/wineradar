    Wineries in Illmitz

    Illmitz has 49 wine producers in our guide, in the Neusiedlersee area. 18 of them welcome visitors, and 2 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Zweigelt, Chardonnay, Scheurebe and Blaufränkisch. Within walking distance there are 12 places to eat and drink and 13 places to stay.
