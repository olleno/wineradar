    Wineries in Traismauer

    Traismauer has 15 wine producers in our guide in the Traisental. 13 of them welcome visitors, and 1 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Grüner Veltliner, Riesling, Gewürztraminer and Roter Veltliner. Within walking distance there are 12 places to eat and drink and 4 places to stay.
