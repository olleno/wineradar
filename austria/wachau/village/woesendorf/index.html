    Wineries in Wösendorf

    Wösendorf has 21 wine producers in our guide in the Wachau. 9 of them welcome visitors, and 1 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Riesling, Grüner Veltliner, Gelber Muskateller and Zweigelt. Within walking distance there are 6 places to eat and drink and 2 places to stay.
