    Grinzing · Wien

    Bioweingut Lenikus

    Bioweingut Lenikus is a wine producer in Grinzing, Vienna (Wien). The estate farms about 25 hectares of vineyard, organically. Its rated wines are mostly white, led by Wiener Gemischter Satz, Grüner Veltliner and Chardonnay. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
