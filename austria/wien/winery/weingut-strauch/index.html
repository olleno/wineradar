    Weingut Strauch

    Weingut Strauch is a wine producer in Strebersdorf, Vienna (Wien). The estate farms about 4.5 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are mostly red, led by St. Laurent, Grüner Veltliner and Blaufränkisch. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
