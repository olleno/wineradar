    Wineries in Stammersdorf

    Stammersdorf has 27 wine producers in our guide in Vienna. 15 of them welcome visitors, and 3 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Chardonnay, Wiener Gemischter Satz, Riesling and Zweigelt. Within walking distance there are 44 places to eat and drink and 0 places to stay.
