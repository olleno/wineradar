    Wineries in Großjedlersdorf

    Großjedlersdorf has 4 wine producers in our guide in Vienna. 3 of them welcome visitors, and 1 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Wiener Gemischter Satz, Weissburgunder, Riesling and Traminer. Within walking distance there are 30 places to eat and drink and 1 places to stay.
