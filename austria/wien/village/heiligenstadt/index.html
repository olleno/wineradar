    Wineries in Heiligenstadt

    Heiligenstadt has 10 wine producers in our guide in Vienna. 9 of them welcome visitors, and 1 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Wiener Gemischter Satz, Riesling, Grüner Veltliner and Chardonnay.
